Scientific Generalizability Notice:

The underlying models were developed using data from six SARI sentinel hospitals in Thailand during 2023–2024. Model performance may change with differences in coding practices, patient populations, circulating pathogens, or healthcare utilization. Periodic local validation is recommended.

About SARI ICD-10 Model v1.0 (Thailand Sentinel Hospital Study)

Methodology, regression equations, and mathematical transparency

This application estimates the expected number of admissions meeting the SARI case definition using age-specific multivariable logistic regression equations developed from sentinel hospital surveillance record reviews in Thailand.

Separate models are strictly applied to pediatric patients aged 0–14 years and adults aged ≥15 years. Individual predicted probabilities are summed to estimate SARI activity during each surveillance period.

Calculation Example (Synthetic Patient Walkthrough)

Patient Profile: Adult, Female, 65 years old, Pneumonia (J18.9), Rainy season (July)
Active Model: Adult Model 2 (ICD-10 + age + sex + season)
--------------------------------------------------
Intercept: -2.721175174
Pneumonia (J18.9) β: +3.158763163
Age β (-0.005659015 × 65): -0.367835975
Female β (Reference): +0.000000000
Rainy season β: -0.203857226
--------------------------------------------------
Linear Predictor η = -0.134105212
Predicted Probability P = 1 / (1 + exp(-η)) = 0.4665 (46.7%)

* This 0.4665 probability is accumulated into the total Estimated SARI sum without threshold dichotomization.
